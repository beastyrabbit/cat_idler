Showing a cat's need on the map

Mallow is at 24 % thirst. Whatever we choose must not turn 31 cats into 31 gauges

Option 1

Ring gauge

A ring around the cat fills with its lowest need; red below 30 %.

Shows how low, not just that it is low

Busy on a crowd; the ring hides feet and cargo

Option 2

Bar under the cat

A small bar beneath the sprite, like a health bar in an RTS.

Universally understood

Looks like combat health; bars on 31 cats become clutter

Option 3 · recommended

Red tick, only when low

Nothing until a need drops below 30 %; then one red dot over the shoulder. The ledger and the Cats sheet carry the numbers.

Quiet map · red means "act" everywhere in the UI

Which need is low needs a click or hover

Option 4

Icon bubble

A speech bubble with the need's icon: a drop for thirst, a bowl for hunger.

Says which need · charming, Sims-like

Competes with the cargo badge that already floats there

Option 5

Hover and selection only

Mallow · thirsty 24 %

The map stays clean; needs appear in a tag on hover and in the ledger when selected.

Cleanest map

Idle players scanning at 8× miss cats in trouble
